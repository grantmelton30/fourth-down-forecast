Add a snap-weighted injury adjustment to the NFL spread

Personnel was invisible to the model outside the QB position: injuries were read only
to demote QB1 to QB2 on the depth chart. This adds a team-level term.

BURDEN IS PLAYING TIME, NOT HEADCOUNT. "Four players out" is meaningless on its own --
four special-teamers and four starters are the same number and a different team. Each
player listed Out or Doubtful is weighted by the share of snaps he had actually been
taking, so one unit is roughly one full-time starter.

Measured on 1,535 walk-forward games against the current model's residual:

  snap-weighted burden differential   -0.518 pts of margin per unit   t = -2.15
  raw count of players out            -0.303 pts per player           t = -2.04
  both together                       neither survives (collinear)

SPREAD ONLY. Against the total residual burden is -0.011 (t = -0.05): a depleted team
scores less and concedes more, and those cancel. Capped at +/-2 points; the worst
observed team-week is 9.8 starter-equivalents, far beyond what a t = -2.15 coefficient
can be trusted to extrapolate.

END-TO-END EFFECT IS SMALL AND THAT IS THE HONEST HEADLINE: spread RMSE 13.7869 ->
13.7702, bias -0.123 -> -0.087. A 0.76-point adjustment cannot move much against a
13.8-point residual. Real, measurable, and nearly irrelevant to accuracy -- kept because
it is correct and because it names a factor in the per-game breakdown, not because it
changes what the model concludes.

THE AS-OF JOIN IS LOAD-BEARING. A player who is Out has no snap-count row that week, so
joining on the exact week matches 0.1% of the report. Joining to his most recent earlier
week gives 78%. The first measurement off the broken join produced a +14.127 coefficient

No lookahead: snap shares are an expanding mean over strictly earlier weeks, and the

Ingest the in-progress season, with caches that can actually expire

  historical caller is untouched. api.live_refresh_hours = 12.

  tag resolve to the newest matching build.
